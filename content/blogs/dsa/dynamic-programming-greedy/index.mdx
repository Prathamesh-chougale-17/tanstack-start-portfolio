## Why DP and Greedy Are Often Confused

Both dynamic programming and greedy try to make optimal decisions, but they do it differently:

- greedy makes the best-looking local choice and never looks back
- DP explores all important states, stores them, and builds the global answer

A good interview skill is knowing when local choice is enough and when you must preserve state.
