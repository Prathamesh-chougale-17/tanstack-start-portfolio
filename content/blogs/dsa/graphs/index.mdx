## Why Graphs Feel Hard

Graphs feel difficult because the same underlying structure can look like:

- cities and roads
- courses and prerequisites
- islands in a matrix
- accounts to merge
- words changing one letter at a time

The trick is to translate the story into:

- nodes
- edges
- traversal
- connectivity
- shortest path
