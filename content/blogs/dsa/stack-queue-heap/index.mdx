## Why These Structures Matter

These patterns all answer one question:

```text
What should be processed next?
```

- stack answers: the **most recent** unresolved item
- queue answers: the **oldest** pending item
- deque answers: whichever end keeps the structure useful
- heap answers: the **best priority** item right now
